About

Žilvinas Kriukovas

I am a physical performance coach specializing in basketball, with experience across professional, youth and national-team basketball.

I have worked in professional basketball in Lithuania and Israel, in youth player development in China, and with Lithuanian youth national teams at the U16 EuroBasket, U17 World Cup and U18 EuroBasket.

My coaching combines strength training, movement skills, speed, jumping and change of direction, prepared for what basketball actually asks of a player. The goal is simple: stronger, faster, more explosive players who are ready to perform.

StrengthForce you can use on the court, built progressively.
Speed & jumpAcceleration, change of direction and vertical power.
ResilienceLoad management and mobility so you stay available.
